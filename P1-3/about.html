About StudentHub

StudentHub Portal is an online platform developed for students and administrators. It provides a single place where students can register, log in, manage their profile, view upcoming college events, read notices, contact the college administration, and submit feedback.

The portal is designed using Semantic HTML5 and Bootstrap to provide a clean, responsive and user-friendly interface.

About Student Hub Portal

Institutional Portal: CHARUSAT Student Information System

Overview & Purpose

Student Hub Portal is a centralized web-based platform designed for CHARUSAT students to access their important academic information in one convenient location.

Purpose

The main purpose of Student Hub is to provide students with an easy way to access their attendance records, assignments, registered courses, weekly timetable, announcements, events, and profile information.

Core Features

✓ Student Dashboard & Overview
✓ Coursework Submissions & Deadlines
✓ Structured Weekly Timetable
✓ Student Profile & Preferences
✓ Real-time Attendance Analytics
✓ Semester Course Catalog & Credits
✓ Live Campus Events Directory
✓ Support Desk & Inquiries

Technologies Used

Developed using HTML5, Bootstrap 5, Vanilla JavaScript, JSON, and PHP with CSV Storage.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

CHARUSAT Student Hub is the integrated university web portal that centralizes student registration, attendance tracking, coursework submissions, campus event participation, and administration services into a single accessible portal.

After logging into your account, navigate to the Profile section from the top navigation bar. You can edit your contact number, emergency contact, and notification preferences, then save changes.

You can submit a message directly via the Contact & Student Support page, or email the university IT Helpdesk at student@charusat.edu.in.
